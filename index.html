SCIENTIFIC MODELS

Efficiency η is a fraction from 0 to 1. Capacitor energy is not the same as energy safely recoverable from an atmospheric discharge.

SAFETY & VALIDITY

IMPORTANT

This module does not connect to lightning, forecast an exact strike, or represent measured atmospheric energy. Never attempt to trigger lightning or connect storage hardware to a real lightning path.

Use only properly isolated, low-voltage bench experiments designed and supervised by qualified personnel. Lightning protection and energy experiments are separate systems.

CAUTION

Engineering assumptions

Calculators use SI units and idealized equations documented in each module. They do not model every conversion loss, material limit, environmental condition, sensor error or system boundary. Outputs are estimates, not field-validated IGERS measurements.

APPLICATION LIMITATIONS

PROTOTYPE
  • The globe is a canvas-based conceptual visualization, not a terrain-accurate 3D GIS globe.
  • NASA Worldview is opened as an external service; embedding may be blocked by the provider or browser.
  • Lightning research visualizer is synthetic. NOAA lightning data ingestion is not implemented.
  • No official emergency warning, SMS, email alert, dispatch or physical equipment control is performed.
  • GitHub Pages is static hosting. This project contains no private backend, authentication, or secret storage.
  • Public data endpoints and browser access can change; deployment cannot guarantee third-party uptime.
