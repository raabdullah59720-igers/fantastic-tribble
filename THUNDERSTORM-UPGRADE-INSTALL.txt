DATA HONESTY / SAFETY
The device image and moving pulse simulation are illustrative. The pulse generator uses editable low-voltage parameters (0–48 V, 0–5 A, up to 1000 ms) and deterministic equations. It does not detect, capture, trigger, or connect to real lightning. Open-Meteo values are forecast/model values, not a direct lightning sensor. No physical equipment is controlled, no SMS or official emergency warning is sent. Never connect this demo to a real lightning conductor or real lightning path.
